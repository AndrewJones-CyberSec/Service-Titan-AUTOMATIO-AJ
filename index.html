Six time-savers in one small panel: notes, job notifications, tech messages, business units, customer texts and moving jobs. It runs from a bookmark and updates itself.

🧰Toolbox ↑Drag it up to your bookmarks bar

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B (Cmd + Shift + B on a Mac). In Safari, choose View, then Show Favorites Bar.
  2. Drag the white Toolbox button onto the bar and let go.
  3. Open the Dispatch board on the Day view and click your new Toolbox bookmark.

What's in the Toolbox

Each tool shows you what it will do before it changes anything, and nothing sends or saves on a timer.

📝

Notes

Ready-made notes in folders (Updates, Techs, Reschedule, plus your own). Click a job and a note, and it types the note on the customer for you to check and save.

🔕

Job Notifications

Add jobs by clicking them, a tech's name, a tag or the whole board. Each shows 🔔 on or 🔕 off, and one button turns them off or back on.

💬

Tech Messages

Send Good Morning, ETA or your own message to the techs you pick. Only teams you approve can be messaged, and you can block anyone by name.

🏢

Business Unit

Switch picked or typed jobs to Mendenhall Branch, or another branch, in one go. Check first without changing anything, and Put back to undo.

📱

Customer Texts

Text customers a saved message: holds, tech updates, reschedules, outreach. 📥 Check replies confirms everyone who answered C and lists the rest.

🔀

Move Jobs

Move jobs to a tech, add or unassign one, or swap two jobs or two techs' whole day without dragging. Review warns about skills and double-booking, and Put back undoes it.

🧭

Tech Router Beta

📞 Fill a spot finds jobs to fill a tech's open time from the Unassigned and Hold lists, with the customer's number to call and move up. 🗺️ Plan the day routes every Unassigned job, 8-12s first, then 12-5s, then 5-9s. Only the kinds of jobs each tech does. Still being built, and it only reads.

Can't drag it? Copy the bookmark instead
  1. Click Copy bookmark below.
  2. Make a new bookmark named Toolbox:
    • Chrome or Edge: right-click the bookmarks bar and choose Add page (Edge: Add favorite).
    • Firefox: right-click the bookmarks toolbar and choose Add Bookmark.
    • Safari: bookmark any page into the Favorites bar, then right-click it and choose Edit Address.
  3. Clear the URL (or Address) box, paste, and save. It should start with javascript:

Pasting it into the address bar won't work: browsers drop the javascript: part there. It has to go in a bookmark.

Updates and problems

The bookmark loads the newest Toolbox every time you click it, so there's never anything to reinstall.

✨ When it updates

The first time you open a new version, a green bar at the top says what changed. Close it with ✕.

⚠️ If ServiceTitan changes something

A yellow bar says which tool is affected. Press Copy report and send it along so it can be fixed. 🩺 Check, at the bottom of the menu, runs the same check any time.
